Verification checklist

  1. Download each generated .jpg above.
  2. Open in macOS Photos (Sonoma+) on an HDR display — highlights should bloom past SDR white on the sky and specular stills; flat midtone must look identical to the SDR-only download.
  3. AirDrop to a recent iPhone, open in iOS Photos — same bloom expectation.
  4. Upload to Google Photos web in Chrome on the same HDR Mac — same bloom expectation.
  5. Chrome direct file open (drag onto a tab) — same bloom on an HDR display.
  6. Run exiftool -G1 -a -s file.jpg and confirm the gain map MPF segment, the XMP hdrgm: block, and the ISO 21496-1 APP2 marker are present.

Pass criteria

  • All four viewers display bloom on the sky and specular stills.
  • All four viewers render the flat midtone identically to its SDR-only twin.
  • exiftool reports MPF + XMP hdrgm + ISO 21496-1 markers with no warnings.
